Hey everyone, I'm Wyatt Wens, the founder and editor of Turnaround California, a small, nonpartisan news site focused on California news and politics.

I started Turnaround California to dig deeper into how our state is run. With the 2026 election just behind us and a new governor taking office in January, it's a big moment to watch how California is governed and what new policies take shape.

I've been fascinated by California politics for years. I've also talked with a lot of Californians who can barely afford to keep building a life in the state they've always called home, and I want to follow how the decisions made in Sacramento play out for people like them.

A little about me: California is the only home I've ever known. I was born in Newport Beach and lived there until I was about 7, when my family moved to the greater Sacramento area. Today I live in Cameron Park. I love this state, but like a lot of Californians, I've found myself asking whether staying here is really worth it.

Have a tip, a question or a correction? Email me. I'd love to hear from you.
