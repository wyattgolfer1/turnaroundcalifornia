Hey everyone, I'm Wyatt Wens, the founder and editor of Turnaround California, a small, nonpartisan news site dedicated to California news and politics.

California is the only home I've ever known. I was born in Newport Beach and lived there until I was about seven, when my family moved to the greater Sacramento area. Today I live in Cameron Park. I love this state, but like a lot of Californians, I've found myself asking whether staying here is really worth it.

I've been fascinated by California politics for years, and that question keeps pulling me back to it. I've talked with so many Californians who can barely afford to keep a life in the place they've always called home. I started Turnaround California to dig deeper into how our state is run, and how the decisions made in Sacramento shape the lives of people like them.

The timing feels important. The 2026 election is behind us, and a new governor takes office in January. I'll be following how the state is run from here, which new policies take shape, and what they mean for everyday Californians.

Have a tip, a question or a correction? Email me. I'd love to hear from you.
